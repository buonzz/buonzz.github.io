I'm a full-stack web developer based in the Philippines. I build websites, apps, and open-source tools — mostly the unglamorous kind that solve one specific, real problem. My work lives at github.com/buonzz.

I don't think of myself as a brilliant coder. My real work is translation: taking a vague product idea and turning it into something that actually ships — then reading the requirement people feel but can't put into words. That part still can't be automated.

This blog is where I think out loud. Some of it is technical — the tools I'm building, the things I broke and fixed. Some of it isn't. I write about psychology, geopolitics, and why people want what they want, because I've never believed those were separate from the work. To build something people use, you first have to understand the people.

A few things I hold

  • School and learning are two different things. I'd rather explore the knowledge graph on my own path.
  • Your situation is yours to take accountability for. That's the moment things start improving.
  • Wealth is not the same as peace of mind. I'd rather have the second one.
  • Competence first, humility second, quiet confidence last. Most loud confidence is a substitute for the homework.

Away from the keyboard, I shoot FPV drone footage and candid photography, edit in DaVinci Resolve, and read more about psychology than is probably healthy. I'm an introvert who prefers one deep conversation to a room full of small talk.

Silent but wild.
